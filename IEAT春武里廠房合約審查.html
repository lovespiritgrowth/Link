三、需調整條款 — 租賃合約 (Lease Agreement)
第 3 條 押金金額過高 + 無息 🔴 高優先
現行條款押金為5個月租金（426,937.50 Baht），無利息，終止後2個月內退還。服務合約另有5個月服務費押金（373,764.40 Baht）。合計 800,701.90 Baht。
問題超過80萬Baht長期凍結，IEAT工業區租約常見水準為3個月押金；無息代表出租方每年免費使用這筆資金。
建議調整兩份合約押金均從5個月降至3個月（可節省約 320,280 Baht）；或要求押金計息（至少按定期存款利率）；押金退還期限從2個月縮短為30天。

📄 原條文 (Clause 3.1 / 3.4)
Clause 3.1 …a security deposit at the amount of Baht 426,937.50 equivalent to Five (5) months of Rental Fee…

Clause 3.4 …returned to the Lessee, without any interest, within two (2) months after the termination…
✏️ 建議修改
Clause 3.1 修改 …a security deposit at the amount of Baht 255,562.50 equivalent to Three (3) months of Rental Fee…

Clause 3.4 修改 …returned to the Lessee, with interest at the prevailing fixed deposit rate of Bangkok Bank, within thirty (30) days after the termination…
第 3.5 條 提前終止 = 押金全數沒收，無對等退出機制 🔴 高優先
現行條款租方提前終止（或因違約被終止），出租方有權沒收全部押金，且不影響其他法律求償權利。合約無任何允許租方提前退出的條款。
問題鴻騰泰國是全新設立公司，業務初期存在不確定性。一旦業務未達預期需提前離場，將損失超過80萬Baht押金且仍可能面臨追訴。
建議調整新增「提前終止條款」：租方提前6個月書面通知，得提前終止合約，沒收押金以剩餘租期比例計算（而非全數），並以此作為出租方唯一救濟途徑。
📄 原條文 vs ✏️ 建議修改條文
📄 原條文 (Clause 3.5)
In the event the Lessee terminates this Agreement prior to the expiration of the lease term…the Lessor shall have the right to forfeit the Security Deposit without prejudice to any right of the Lessor in respect of any prior breach hereof.

[無任何租方主動終止機制]
✏️ 建議新增 Clause 3.6
Clause 3.6 (新增) Early Termination by Lessee:
The Lessee may terminate this Agreement by giving the Lessor not less than six (6) months' prior written notice. Upon such early termination, the Lessor shall forfeit the Security Deposit on a pro-rata basis: (Remaining months ÷ Total lease months) × Security Deposit amount. The refund balance shall be returned within 30 days. This shall be the Lessor's sole and exclusive remedy for early termination.
第 4.14 條 永久裝修到期歸出租方，租方無補償 🔴 高優先
現行條款租方在廠房內安裝的「永久性裝修、固定設施」，合約到期後自動成為出租方財產；租方不得拆除、不得求償，除非事先取得出租方書面同意。
問題鴻騰泰國將投入大量製造設備、生產線裝修（沖壓機底座、接地系統、輸送線固定架等）。「永久性」定義模糊，合約到期後可能全部歸出租方，損失難以估計。
建議調整明確界定「永久性裝修」排除「製造設備及安裝基礎」；加入條款允許租方到期前選擇拆除並修復原狀，或留下並獲合理補償（按折舊後殘值評估）。
📄 原條文 vs ✏️ 建議修改條文
📄 原條文 (Clause 4.14)
…the Lessee agrees that any modifications, fixtures and fittings installed or erected in a permanent manner in the Premises shall become the property of the Lessor on termination of this Agreement, and the Lessee is not entitled to demolish or remove them nor claim any compensation for their loss, unless prior consent has been granted by the Lessor…
✏️ 建議修改
…any structural modifications to walls, floors, ceilings, and built-in utility systems shall become the property of the Lessor on termination, provided that:
(a) Manufacturing equipment, machinery, installation bases, and removable fixtures shall remain the Lessee's property and may be removed with proper restoration of the Premises;
(b) For fixtures agreed to remain, the Lessor shall pay the Lessee fair compensation based on depreciated value, as jointly assessed.
第 4.6 條 結構性修繕義務不當落在租方 🔴 高優先
現行條款租方須自費維持廠房內外良好狀態，包括「結構性或其他損壞（structural or otherwise）」之修繕與替換。
問題「結構性損壞」應屬出租方之範疇（第5.7條規定出租方負責主結構），兩條條款相互矛盾且對租方不利。製造業用途很可能因設備震動造成地板、牆壁輕微損壞，被出租方歸咎於租方。
建議調整刪除「structural or otherwise」，改為「因租方使用不當造成之內部非結構性損壞」。並於第5.7條明確：主結構（屋頂、外牆、地基、樑柱）之修繕由出租方負擔。
📄 原條文 vs ✏️ 建議修改條文
📄 原條文 (Clause 4.6)
…maintain in a good and tenantable condition the exterior and interior of the Premises…including repair and/or replacement of all damage, structural or otherwise, and repair and/or replacement of closets, sinks, pipes, windows, doors, locks…arising out of the Lessee's use of the Premises…
✏️ 建議修改
…maintain in a good and tenantable condition the interior of the Premises…including repair and/or replacement of non-structural damage to closets, sinks, pipes, windows, doors, locks…arising out of the Lessee's negligent or improper use of the Premises.

For the avoidance of doubt, structural repairs (main structure, roof, external walls, floor slabs, drainage) shall be the Lessor's responsibility per Clause 5.7.
第 5.2 / 4.9 條 保險費由租方全付，但受益人為出租方 🔴 高優先
現行條款出租方以「出租方名義」投保廠房火災及相關保險，出租方為唯一受益人，但全部保費由租方負擔。出租方亦可要求租方因業務性質付加保費。
問題租方承擔全部保費（估35,000 Baht/年以上），但保險理賠金全歸出租方。若發生火災，廠房由保險修復，但租方設備、成品、原材料損失完全無保障。
建議調整要求以「共同被保險人（co-insured）」形式納入租方；保費應按保障比例分攤或由出租方主要承擔；加保費須事先通知並取得租方書面同意。
📄 原條文 vs ✏️ 建議修改條文
📄 原條文 (Clause 5.2 / 4.9)
Clause 5.2 The Premises shall be insured by the Lessor…provided that the Lessor shall be specified as the beneficiary, and the Lessee shall be responsible for the payment of all insurance premiums.

Clause 4.9(a) to pay any increased or extra insurance premium or surcharge levied by insurance providers attributed to the specific risks associated with the Lessee's business.
✏️ 建議修改
Clause 5.2 修改 The Premises shall be insured by the Lessor…provided that both the Lessor and the Lessee shall be named as co-insured and co-beneficiaries for their respective insurable interests. The cost of insurance premiums shall be borne by the Lessor. Any additional premiums directly attributable to the Lessee's specific business activities shall be borne by the Lessee, subject to the Lessee's prior written approval.
第 2.5 條 續租漲幅5%/年，計算方式矛盾 🟡 中優先
現行條款續租時租金「以5%每年」的速度調漲，但同一條款又說「最終金額雙方協議決定」——兩者矛盾。
問題若按年複利5%計算，3年續租期累計漲幅達15.76%；且「雙方再協議」等於沒有保障，若出租方要求更高漲幅，租方缺乏議價依據。
建議調整明確限定為每年單純增加3%（非複利），或與泰國CPI通膨指數連動，設定上限；刪除「雙方協議最終金額」的模糊空間。
📄 原條文 vs ✏️ 建議修改條文
📄 原條文 (Clause 2.5)
…the rental fee for the said renewed Agreement shall be increased at the rate of (5%) percent per annum of the Rental Fee specified in Clause 2.4 above. The final rental increment amount shall be mutually agreed upon.
✏️ 建議修改
…the rental fee for the renewed term shall be increased by not more than three percent (3%) per annum, simple (non-compounding), of the Rental Fee in effect at the end of the original term, or the increase in CPI as published by the Bank of Thailand for the preceding twelve (12) months, whichever is lower.
第 2.4 條 房屋稅、地方稅全轉嫁租方，未設上限 🟡 中優先
現行條款房屋稅、地方發展稅及未來新增稅費（包含未來增加金額），全部由租方負擔，收到帳單後30天內繳付。
問題報價估算90,000 Baht/年，但合約無上限約定。若政府調整工業區稅率，租方需承擔所有增加，增加了不確定的成本風險。
建議調整在合約中明訂租方負擔稅費的上限金額（以90,000 Baht/年為基準，超出部分由出租方承擔）；或明確僅「使用性質導致的附加稅」才轉嫁，建築物法定稅負由出租方自行負擔。
📄 原條文 vs ✏️ 建議修改條文
📄 原條文 (Clause 2.4)
…the Lessee shall pay or reimburse the Lessor, the amounts assessed from time to time (including future increases) in respect of house and land tax, local development tax and other taxes or charges which may come into force with respect to the Premises after the date of this Agreement…
✏️ 建議修改
…the Lessee shall pay or reimburse the Lessor in respect of house and land tax and local development tax, subject to a maximum aggregate amount of Baht 90,000 (Ninety Thousand Baht) per annum ("Tax Cap"). Any amount exceeding the Tax Cap shall be borne by the Lessor. The Tax Cap shall be reviewed and mutually agreed in writing at each renewal.
第 2.3 條 續租通知期90天，錯過即喪失權利 🟡 中優先
現行條款租方須在到期前90天（即2029年5月2日前）發出書面通知，否則自動喪失續租權利，無寬限期規定。
問題3年後的時間點難以預測，若因海外行政作業延誤或疏漏，單純錯過截止日即喪失整個續租選擇權，對外資租方風險較高。
建議調整通知期從90天縮短至60天；並加入：若租方未於期限內通知，出租方須再書面提醒，租方有15天寬限期。

📄 原條文 (Clause 2.3)

[無寬限期，無出租方提醒義務]
✏️ 建議修改
…the Lessee shall, no later than sixty (60) days before the expiration of the original term, give written notice to the Lessor of its intent to renew this Agreement.

If the Lessee fails to give such notice within the said period, the Lessor shall notify the Lessee in writing within seven (7) days. The Lessee shall then have an additional fifteen (15) days grace period to exercise its renewal right.
第 4.5 條 公共事業費7天付款期太緊 🟡 中優先
現行條款若水電費帳單開在出租方名下，出租方向租方轉開發票，租方須在收到後7天內付款，否則計2%/月罰息。
問題跨國付款作業流程（匯款、財務審核、銀行作業天數）通常需要7-14天，7天期限對海外管理的外資企業不現實。
建議調整付款期從7天延長為21天；罰息觸發點改為逾期14天；罰息率從2%/月降至1%/月。

第 8.4 條 租方預先放棄一切訴訟權利 🟡 中優先
現行條款若租方逾期未交還廠房，出租方可強制進入、移除員工及財物，租方提前放棄對出租方提出侵入、損毀、侵害人身自由等一切民刑事訴訟的權利。
問題此條款過度剝奪租方法律救濟權，若出租方行使不當（如誤入、暴力移除），租方完全無法主張任何法律保障。
建議調整刪除「放棄一切民刑事訴訟權利」的寬泛措辭；改為：若租方無故逾期不交廠超過X天，出租方可依法律程序收回。

第 9.3 條 出租方可無限制轉讓，租方無退出選項 🟢 低優先

問題REIT可能被併購或資產出售，若新出租方管理風格截然不同，租方權益可能受影響，且租方毫無退出選項。
建議調整若出租方發生所有權重大變更（>50%），租方應有權在30天內選擇是否繼續，若選擇終止則押金無損失全額退還。

四、需調整條款 — 服務合約 (Service Agreement)
服務 第3.1 vs 3.5條 服務費含VAT與否 — 合約自相矛盾 🔴 高優先（簽約前必須確認）
現行條款服務合約第3.1條載明月服務費為 74,752.88 Baht；第3.5條卻說「服務費不含VAT，租方須另繳7%增值稅」。
問題74,752.88 = 69,862.50 × 1.07（含7% VAT）。若按第3.5條「不含VAT」解讀，月服務費實際為 74,752.88 × 1.07 = 79,985 Baht，3年多付約 18.8萬 Baht。
建議調整簽約前要求出租方確認：74,752.88 Baht 為含稅金額，並修改第3.5條刪除「exclusive of VAT」措辭，或明確標示稅前/稅後金額。
📄 原條文矛盾對照 vs ✏️ 建議修改條文
📄 原條文（兩條矛盾）
Clause 3.1（金額） Aug 1, 2026 – Jul 31, 2029 → 74,752.88 Baht/month

Clause 3.5（VAT說明） The Service Fee…are exclusive of Value Added Tax ("VAT") and the Lessee shall pay any VAT…

→ 矛盾：74,752.88 已 = 69,862.50 × 1.07（含稅），但條文說不含稅
✏️ 建議修改 Clause 3.5
The Service Fee of Baht 74,752.88 per month as specified in Clause 3.1 is inclusive of Value Added Tax (VAT) at the rate of 7%. The VAT component amounts to Baht 4,890.38 per month (base fee before VAT: Baht 69,862.50 per month).

（刪除原 "exclusive of VAT" 措辭及後續「租方須另繳VAT」的要求）
服務 第9.2條 交叉違約 — 服務費延誤可致整個租約即時終止 🔴 高優先
現行條款「任何服務合約違約視為租賃合約違約；任何租賃合約違約視為服務合約違約。任一合約終止，另一合約自動同步即時終止，無需另行通知。」
問題服務費僅約74,752 Baht，若因銀行作業或帳務錯誤導致單月服務費逾期，理論上出租方可據此終止整個租賃合約，影響遠大於違約原因本身。
建議調整取消全面交叉違約機制；或縮小至「重大且持續超過60天的違約」才觸發交叉終止；服務費逾期不得單獨觸發租賃合約終止。
📄 原條文 vs ✏️ 建議修改條文
📄 原條文 (Service Clause 9.2)
Both parties agree that any breach of this Agreement shall be regarded as a breach of the Lease Agreement and any breach of the Lease Agreement shall be regarded as a breach of this Agreement. Further, if this Agreement is terminated for whatever reason, the Lease Agreement shall be concurrently terminated forthwith without notice, and if the Lease Agreement is terminated for whatever reason, this Agreement shall be concurrently terminated forthwith without notice.
✏️ 建議修改
A breach of this Agreement shall only constitute a breach of the Lease Agreement, and vice versa, where such breach is material and remains uncured for a period of sixty (60) days following written notice from the non-breaching party.

For the avoidance of doubt, a payment default of the Service Fee of less than thirty (30) days shall not constitute a material breach sufficient to trigger termination of the Lease Agreement. Concurrent termination shall require a separate written notice specifying the grounds for termination of each agreement.
服務 第3.1條 空置期間服務費仍須全額繳付 🟡 中優先
現行條款「無論租方是否進駐，服務費均須按時全額支付（whether the Lessee is in occupation or not）。」
問題鴻騰泰國設廠初期（取得BOI、公司設立、設備進口）可能有2-6個月空置期。空置期間每月仍須付74,752 Baht服務費，增加初期資金壓力。
建議調整要求加入「裝修寬限期（Fit-Out Period）」：自合約生效起前3個月，若廠房尚未開始生產作業，服務費減收50%。
📄 原條文 vs ✏️ 建議修改條文
📄 原條文 (Service Clause 3.1 末段)
The Lessee shall pay the Service Fee, whether formally demanded or not, throughout the entire term of the Lease Agreement, whether the Lessee is in occupation or not.
✏️ 建議修改
The Lessee shall pay the Service Fee throughout the entire term of the Lease Agreement.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, for the first three (3) months from the commencement date ("Fit-Out Period"), the Service Fee shall be reduced by fifty percent (50%) provided the Lessee has not yet commenced commercial operations. The Lessee shall notify the Lessor in writing upon commencement of commercial operations.
服務 第3.3條 服務押金沒收條件絕對，無比例計算 🟡 中優先
現行條款「若租方違約或在服務期滿前終止合約，押金不予退還（shall not be refunded）」——相比租賃合約「可選擇沒收」，這裡是「一定沒收」，更加絕對。
問題提前終止時服務押金（373,764 Baht）必然全數沒收，與租賃押金合計超過80萬，損失過重且無任何緩衝機制。
建議調整與租賃合約押金條款統一：提前終止時按剩餘月數比例計算沒收金額，而非全數沒收。
